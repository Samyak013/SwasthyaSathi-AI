AI-powered healthcare platform that fetches all user data from ABHA cards, enabling doctors and patients to manage health records with multilingual AI chatbot, diagnosis assistance, and consent-based record sharing

ABHA-based authentication - Users login/register using ABHA ID with Aadhaar OTP, fetch all profile data from ABHA system

Role-based profile completion - After ABHA login, users select role (Doctor/Patient/Pharmacy) and add role-specific details only
Doctor profile extension - Add specialization, experience, hospital/clinic name, HPR ID (Health Professional Registry)
Patient profile extension - Medical conditions, allergies, emergency contacts auto-linked to ABHA health records
Pharmacy profile extension - License number, location, operating hours, medicine stock database
Doctor Dashboard - Search and view patients by ABHA ID, see complete ABHA-linked health history and records
AI-powered Digital Prescription System - Generate prescriptions with AI drug interaction checks, auto-share via ABHA consent system
Patient Dashboard - Timeline view of all ABHA health records, prescriptions, lab reports with AI-generated summaries
AI Multilingual Health Chatbot - Answer health questions in English/Hindi/Marathi, explain medical reports and prescriptions using OpenAI
AI Diagnosis Assistant - Doctors input symptoms, AI suggests conditions, recommended tests, and treatment options based on medical databases
ABHA Consent-Based Record Sharing - Patients grant consent for doctors to access their complete ABHA health history
Medicine & Appointment Reminders - Smart AI-scheduled notifications based on prescription data and appointment history
Pharmacy Prescription Verification - Scan QR codes from ABHA-signed prescriptions, verify authenticity, update dispensed status
Firebase Firestore Database - Store app-specific data (prescriptions, reminders, chat history) synced with ABHA records
Real-time Doctor-Patient Chat - Secure messaging with AI translation between English, Hindi, and Marathi
Health Analytics Dashboard - AI visualizations showing patient trends, disease patterns, risk predictions from ABHA data
Emergency SOS System - One-click alert with ABHA profile, vitals, location sent to nearest registered hospitals
